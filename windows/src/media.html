Tablet camera and microphone

On the tablet, open Perspective USB Bridge and tap Share camera & microphone, then switch to Parsec. The address is shown on that card.

Pick a virtual audio device (BlackHole on Mac, VB-CABLE on Windows), then choose that same device as the microphone in Zoom, Teams or FaceTime. Avoid your speakers: Parsec would send your own voice back to the tablet.
